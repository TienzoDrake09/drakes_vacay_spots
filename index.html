Puerto Galera

White Beach

I recommend Puerto Galera because it offers beautiful beaches, clear waters, and a relaxing tropical atmosphere perfect for a getaway. You can also enjoy activities like snorkeling and exploring its coral reefs.

Boracay

White Beach

I recommend Boracay because of its stunning white-sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, and lively atmosphere. You can also partake in parasailing and riding a traditional paraw sailboat.

Taal Volcano

Tourist Attraction

Because of its breathtaking scenery overlooking the lake and unique volcanic landscape, making it a great destination for adventure and sightseeing. of course only when not active :D
